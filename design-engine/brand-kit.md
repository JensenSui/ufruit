## Brand Identity

**Brand Name:** 優 FRÜIT (U Fruit / 優 Fruit)

**What the brand does:**  
A bold, vibrant fresh-fruit retail brand delivering artisan-quality, hand-picked produce to everyday consumers who care about freshness, colour, and joy.

**Industry:** Food & Beverage / Fresh Produce Retail

**Primary Audience:**  
Health-conscious consumers aged 20–45, families, foodies, and lifestyle-driven shoppers who buy premium fresh fruit in-store and online. Values freshness, visual appeal, and natural living.

---

## Brand Personality

**Adjectives (3–5):** Fresh, Bold, Vibrant, Artisanal, Joyful

**Tone of Voice:**  
Warm and energetic, not corporate. Direct and clear — no jargon. Celebratory of nature. Speaks like a passionate market vendor who genuinely loves what they sell.

**Brands we admire (visual reference):**  
Eataly, Whole Foods (market energy), Aesop (craft/artisan feel), Nothing (bold typography + colour)

**Brands we want to differentiate from:**  
Avoid looking like generic supermarket produce departments — too sterile and price-driven. Not Woolworths/Coles generic. We are playful and handcrafted, not clinical.

**Colour Notes:**
- The four logo colours (Red, Orange, Amber, Green) should never appear stacked — always spread across elements to preserve the rainbow effect of the brand.
- Warm Cream `#F5F0E8` is the preferred background — not pure white. Use it on all marketing collateral.
- Golden Amber `#F0B429` is the primary CTA button colour — high visibility, warm energy.
- Never use Primary Red directly on a dark background without white separation.
- Forest Olive `#4A6B2A` pairs with Cream for a premium natural feel.
- Do NOT use cold blues, purples, or greys — they contradict the brand's warmth.

## Typography

**Display / Heading Font:** Outfit  
- Weight: Bold (700) / Extrabold (800)  
- Source: Google Fonts — `https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Outfit:wght@400;500;600;700;800&display=swap`  
- Use for: Hero headlines, brand taglines, large poster type, section titles  
- Character: Sleek, geometric, sophisticated — echoes the sculptural curves of the canonical UFRÜIT V3 logo

**Body / Copy Font:** Plus Jakarta Sans  
- Weight: Regular (400) / Medium (500)  
- Source: Google Fonts — `https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Plus+Jakarta+Sans:wght@400;500;600;700&display=swap`  
- Use for: Sub-headlines, lead copy, card descriptions, paragraph text, navigation  
- Character: Ultra-clean Swiss modernism, high legibility, whisper-quiet luxury

**Accent / Label Font:** Outfit (Semi-Bold 600 / Bold 700)  
- Source: Google Fonts  
- Use for: Category labels, price tags, spaced-out subheadings, captions, badges  
- Character: Structured, clean, modern

**Typography Rules:**
- Headlines: Outfit (700/800), always.
- Subheadings / taglines: Outfit (600) or Plus Jakarta Sans (500).
- Body copy: Plus Jakarta Sans (400/500).
- Never use more than 2 fonts in a single design.
- The FRÜIT wordmark itself uses multi-colour hand-painted brush lettering — never recreate it as live type; always use the logo asset.

## Logo

**Logo Files Location:** `assets/logos/`

### 1. Primary Master Logo (Dual Version: Mandarin & English)
The Primary Master Logo features the multi-colour brush "FRÜIT" wordmark layered across a bold, architectural black symbol in the background.
- **Mandarin Master (`優 FRÜIT`):** Uses stylized geometric shapes forming the Mandarin character "優" (*Yōu* = Excellent/Superior), phonetically sounding like "U".
- **English Master (`U FRÜIT`):** Replaces the Mandarin character with a large, bold, architectural English alphabet capital **"U"**, making the "U Fruit" phonetic connection immediate for Western/English-speaking markets while retaining the layered master logo architecture.
- **Usage:** Maximum impact collateral (e.g., hero brand moments, signage, uniform backs, packaging).
- **Source Files:**
  - `früit logo.pdf` (in root for Mandarin Master)
  - `design-engine/assets/logos/fruit-logo-english-master-v1.jpg` (English Master — Warm Cream BG)
  - `design-engine/assets/logos/fruit-logo-english-master-v2.jpg` (English Master — Pure White BG)
- **Print Note:** Ensure the background "優" or "U" symbol is printed in Rich Black (C75 M68 Y67 K90), not standard #000000.

### 2. Secondary Logo: FRÜIT SELECTION (Simplified)
The secondary logo removes the large black background character/letter and instead features the word "SELECTION" underneath the brush "FRÜIT".
- **Usage:** Small-scale applications (e.g., front chest embroidery), when the background is already complex, or when "SELECTION" is necessary for context.
- **Source File:** `color pallete.png` (in root)

**Logo Rules:**
- Minimum clear space: equal to the height of the "F" on all four sides.
- Never stretch, skew, recolour, or add drop shadows to the logo.
- **Spacing:** While the Primary Logo is visually complex, the space *around* it must remain clean and empty (Warm Cream) to allow it to breathe and avoid overcrowding.
- Never place the multi-colour logo on a heavily coloured background — it needs Cream or White behind it.
- Never recreate the brush lettering in a different font — the logo is an asset, always use the file.

## Visual Style

**Photography Style:**  
Natural light. Clean marble or stone surfaces. Styled produce arranged with intention — not chaotic. Fresh water droplets visible. Warm ambient light (not flash). Real fruits, no CGI. Hero angle: angled overhead or 45° product shot. Colour-rich arrangements — lean into the brand palette naturally.

Reference: `assets/hero.png`, `assets/juice.png`, `assets/whole-fruit.png`

**Illustration / Graphic Style:**  
Flat 2D — no drop shadows on graphic elements. Brush-painted textures are on-brand (referencing the logo lettering). Geometric shapes allowed for layout structure. Organic, rounded forms preferred over sharp angles.

**Patterns / Textures:**
- Subtle grain/noise overlay (5–10% opacity) on solid colour backgrounds for depth.
- Warm Cream `#F5F0E8` flat wash as primary background — no gradients on BG.
- Optional: thin rule lines in Forest Olive for editorial dividers.

**Iconography Style:**  
Outline icons, 1.5–2px stroke, rounded caps and joins. Colour: match surrounding text colour. Size: consistent optical sizing across sets.

---

## Design Don'ts

- ❌ No gradients on or around the logo
- ❌ No drop shadows on text elements
- ❌ No cold colours (blue, purple, grey) — they kill the brand warmth
- ❌ No pure black (`#000000`) — use Charcoal `#1A1A18` instead
- ❌ No pure white backgrounds — use Warm Cream `#F5F0E8`
- ❌ No stock photo-style food imagery (people smiling at salads, studio-lit fake styling)
- ❌ Never recreate the FRÜIT brush lettering in another font
- ❌ Never use all four logo colours simultaneously on UI elements — causes visual noise
- ❌ No Comic Sans, Papyrus, or display fonts other than Outfit/Plus Jakarta Sans
- ❌ No overcrowded layouts — this brand breathes and lets the fruit speak
